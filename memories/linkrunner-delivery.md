For Linkrunner dashboard and website changes, worktree identity and dev-server ownership are first-class checks. A preview is not reliable until the process, checkout, route, and relevant service health are verified.

When changing public website routes, carry through redirects, metadata, structured data, sitemap entries, and related discoverability artifacts. For dashboard work, avoid interactive lint setup when a focused type check, test, or build is more appropriate.
