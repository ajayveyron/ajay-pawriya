FramerBot is an AI-native dashboard for editing Framer CMS content outside the Framer editor.

- Started as an internal solution to a small CMS workflow problem.
- Was later opened for broader use.
- Represents an approach of turning a narrow operational friction point into a simple product.

Use this as project context, not as a current feature specification.
